1. Program Description

Rooftop Heroes LLC operates a 1:1 transactional SMS program exclusively for service-related communications tied to a confirmed roofing service request that you have submitted. Messages are sent only after you have provided express written or recorded consent.

Message types we send

  • Service request confirmations
  • Inspection scheduling and appointment confirmations
  • Appointment reminders and updates
  • Estimate delivery and follow-up
  • Project status updates
  • Customer support responses related to your active service request

Message types we never send

  • Promotional, marketing, or advertising messages
  • Messages used for lead generation or re-engagement
  • Messages to individuals who have not provided prior express written consent
  • Messages to individuals who have opted out

2. Consent

You must provide express written consent before we send you any SMS message. Consent is obtained via:

  • Checking the SMS consent checkbox on our website contact form at the time of submitting a service request
  • Verbal recorded consent provided during an inbound phone call to our office

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You may receive our roofing services without agreeing to receive SMS messages.

We record the date, time, source, and consent language at the time consent is provided. These records are retained for compliance purposes.

3. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us.

After you send STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent to your number unless you re-consent.

You may also opt out by contacting us directly at (734) 201-1000 or info@rooftopheroes.com.
